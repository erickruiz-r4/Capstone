Through The Room

"Through The Room" is a contemporary art business founded on September 10, 2025, that specializes in commercial screen printing while embracing a broad spectrum of artistic mediums. Everything thing is down in home, meaning that it's literally through the room. Finding ways to make complex designs with the limited space and equipment. The business not only excels in screen printing but also incorporates diverse art form such as welding, sculpting, ceramics, painting, photography, and graphic design. This home-based venture embodies a commitment to creativity and innovation, making it a unique blend of art and craftsmanship.
